✦ English

2

✦ English

✦ English

✦ English

✦ English

✦ English

✦ English

10

✦ English

✦ English

1

✦ English
They kept also the feast of tabernacles, as it is written, and offered the daily burnt offerings by number, according to the custom, as the duty of every day required;

✦ English
